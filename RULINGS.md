# Dial contract rulings

### RL-1 — Existing capabilities and future routing analytics

Date: 19 October 2026. Ruled by ct, standing pattern 1; applied to INTEG-006 and ROUTE-001 on 28 October 2026.

The standing ruling permits completion when every existing capability conforms
and named future clauses inherit the obligation. INTEG-006 keeps its complete
booking analytics acceptance. ROUTE-001 carries the same duty for its unbuilt
routing forms: configured-only tags, applicable events exactly once, privacy
disclosure, parent events for embeds and click-only conversions without answers.
Its public journey must prove that inherited duty before routing forms ship.
No acceptance sentence is removed or weakened, and no provider attribution or
live advertising account is claimed. Owners act for admins until OPER-001, as
that clause already states.
