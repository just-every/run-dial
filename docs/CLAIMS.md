# Implemented contract claims

PAY-001 is done on the native single-host surface: a host connects their own Stripe account by Connect Standard OAuth (signed, session-bound, single-use ten-minute state), sets a USD price or a price per duration, and the invitee pays through Stripe Checkout while the slot is held for ten minutes through the BOOK-002 claim. The booking confirms only on Stripe's signed event; the return URL, duplicates, bad signatures and foreign accounts confirm nothing. Receipt, manage page, mail and the operator record show the amount and Stripe reference; cancel and reschedule keep the payment, and any refund or fee follows PAY-003. A Stripe test-mode run paid test card 4242 on Stripe's hosted page against a Checkout Session Dial created; the test harness then fetched the real `checkout.session.completed` from Stripe's events API and delivered it to Dial, adding the connected-account field and signing it with a throwaway endpoint's secret. Stripe-to-Dial delivery to a deployed Worker and a real Connect OAuth grant are not claimed. Packages and coupons, and reconciliation remain PAY-004 and PAY-006.

Evidence: `tests/payments-wire.test.mjs`, `tests/booking-wire/payment-journey.mjs`, `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs`, migration `0068_stripe_payments.sql`, screenshots and `stripe-test-mode.json` in `evidence/contract/2026-10-21/PAY-001/`.

AVAIL-012 is done on the native single-host surface: locked location time and the invitee browser observation stay distinct from request submission through booking, confirmation, manage, export and restore. Operator-created bookings without an invitee observation explicitly say “Zone not provided by invitee.” Existing SMS and workflow-variable obligations remain inherited by FLOW-002 and FLOW-001.

Evidence: `tests/booking-wire/booking-policy-journey.mjs`, `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs`, `tests/archive-prior-release.test.mjs`, migration `0067_request_browser_timezone.sql` and screenshots in `evidence/contract/2026-10-21/AVAIL-012/`.

PAY-003 is done on the native single-host surface: an event type can take a fixed or percentage deposit with the balance due a set number of hours before the start and paid through its own 256-bit balance link, and can state a free cancellation window with a late-cancellation fee and a no-show fee. The booking step and manage page state the deposit, the balance and its due time and the policy, and the confirmation the deposit, the balance and its due time; a fee needs the invitee's unchecked consent, recorded with its wording version, time and amounts, and a card saved by Stripe (a SetupIntent when nothing is due at booking). Cancelling inside the free window refunds everything paid automatically and once; after it the stated fee is charged once to the saved card, and a declined charge is recorded failed and never retried. Hosts refund in full or in part with a reason, and charge the no-show fee after the no-show mark, from the booking record. Every movement records its actor, amount, reason, time, Stripe reference and idempotency key; while Stripe is not connected, a host refund is refused and an automatic refund is recorded failed. When the host cancels a paid booking from its record, or the host's Google Calendar deletion or an expired host reschedule request cancels it, Dial refunds everything captured in full and once, charges no fee and voids the open balance Checkout or Stripe invoice; the operator may refund less, or nothing, only with a reason, which the invitee's cancellation email states with the amount, and a balance or invoice Stripe captured after the cancellation is recorded and refunded in full once. No real Stripe account or key was used: Stripe is synthetic at the HTTP edge.

Evidence: `tests/payments-wire.test.mjs` (`tests/booking-wire/payment-terms-journey.mjs`), `tests/scheduling-report.test.mjs`, `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs`, migration `0069_deposits_refunds_fees.sql` and the 1440 and 390 screen proof `scripts/verify-money-screens.mjs`.

PAY-005 is done on the native single-host surface: a host turns on Stripe Tax and sets the business name in the payments settings. With tax on, the booking step takes a US billing address and shows Stripe Tax's sales tax before anything is held; Checkout charges exactly that tax, a non-taxable address pays none, and a confirmation whose tax differs from what was shown is refused. Every payment (upfront, a deposit's balance, an invoice or a saved-card fee) sends one receipt with the business name, amount, tax and Stripe reference to the invitee and the booking's hosts only, and the receipt page and booking record show the same figures. Hosts invoice an unpaid booking from its record on a Stripe-hosted invoice that is marked paid only by Stripe's signed event; a cancelled or upfront-paid booking cannot be invoiced, cancelling voids the open invoice, and an invoice paid for a cancelled booking is recorded and refunded in full once. Refunds return tax in proportion. Dial keeps tax amounts and the jurisdiction summary, never an address or card. No real Stripe account or key was used, and Dial does not file Stripe Tax transactions.

Evidence: `tests/payments-wire.test.mjs` (`tests/booking-wire/payment-tax-journey.mjs`), `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs`, migration `0070_sales_tax_receipts_invoices.sql` and the 1440 and 390 screen proof `scripts/verify-money-screens.mjs`.

REPORT-001 is partial on the single-host surface: Bookings has a scheduling report for a chosen period and event type with bookings created, rescheduled and canceled (with reasons), attendance and no-show rates, lead time, popular days and hours in the host's zone, bookings by UTM and source and by duration, and revenue, fees, refunds and net. Every figure states its definition and opens the bookings list on exactly the bookings it counts. Host and team choice, routing-form conversion, round-robin distribution and a member seeing only their own figures wait for the clauses that build teams, routing forms and roles.

Evidence: `tests/scheduling-report.test.mjs` with its seeded month in `tests/review/report-month.ts`, and the 1440 and 390 screen proof `scripts/verify-report-screens.mjs`.

EVENT-009 is done on the native single-host surface: a host allows a configured number of guests, the invitee adds and removes them from the booking and manage steps, and guests receive calendar invitations and Dial confirmation and change notices without the invitee's manage link. Removing a guest sends one cancellation; guest erasure removes their entries and notices while preserving the invitee booking. Calendar and mail providers are synthetic at the HTTP edge. `tests/booking-wire/guest-journey.mjs`, `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs` and `scripts/verify-guest-screens.mjs` carry the native, archive, prior-schema and 1440/390 screen proof.

EVENT-008 is partial: a free event without saved-card fees can require approval, hold a slot for a configurable window (24 hours by default), and show the invitee its pending state. The owner approves or declines from Bookings. Approval alone confirms and creates calendar delivery and reminders; decline and expiry release the slot and tell the invitee once. Requests and decisions reach only the invitee and the event host recipients. PAY-007 withdraws that refusal for upfront/deposit prices without saved-card fees and for free saved-card-fee types; combined priced/deposit-plus-fee types retain the named refusal in settings and at request. Optional tentative calendar display, direct decisions from mail and native mobile approval remain open. Native, archive, upgrade, erasure and 1440/390 screen proof is in `tests/booking-wire/approval-journey.mjs`, `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs` and `scripts/verify-approval-screens.mjs`.

PAY-007 is partial: upfront and deposit approval uses cards/Link manual authorization of the shown total within ten minutes. Approval requests capture; only the matching signed capture event confirms the booking, once, under the existing signature, timestamp and connected-account checks. Decline, expiry and host cancellation void the authorization and release the slot after Stripe acknowledges it, with one notice. Windows default to 24 hours and refuse more than seven days. Deposits authorize only the deposit and its shown tax, with a balance link after capture. Free saved-card-fee approval uses fresh SetupIntent consent, no preapproval charge and detachment on decline, expiry or cancellation. Frozen amounts, one confirmation per recipient, consented fees, refunds and actor/amount/reason/processor accounting are asserted. Combined price/deposit plus saved-card-fee approval remains refused; its combined authorization/SetupIntent and capture-or-void/detach cycle and sentences 1–9 remain unproved. Stripe stays synthetic at the HTTP edge; live capture, void and webhook delivery to a deployed Dial are unobserved. Evidence: `tests/booking-wire.test.mjs` (EVENT-008/PAY-007 approval requests authorize shown terms and confirm only after host approval and signed capture), `tests/booking-wire/paid-approval-journey.mjs` labels 1–9, `tests/booking-wire/paid-approval-movements.mjs`, `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs`, `tests/booking-wire/paid-approval-screens.mjs` and migration `migrations/core/0075_paid_approval.sql`.
