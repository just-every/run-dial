# Implemented contract claims

PAY-001 is done on the native single-host surface: a host connects their own Stripe account by Connect Standard OAuth (signed, session-bound, single-use ten-minute state), sets a USD price or a price per duration, and the invitee pays through Stripe Checkout while the slot is held for ten minutes through the BOOK-002 claim. The booking confirms only on Stripe's signed event; the return URL, duplicates, bad signatures and foreign accounts confirm nothing. Receipt, manage page, mail and the operator record show the amount and Stripe reference; cancel and reschedule keep the payment and say Dial issues no refund. A Stripe test-mode run paid test card 4242 on Stripe's hosted page against a Checkout Session Dial created; the test harness then fetched the real `checkout.session.completed` from Stripe's events API and delivered it to Dial, adding the connected-account field and signing it with a throwaway endpoint's secret. Stripe-to-Dial delivery to a deployed Worker and a real Connect OAuth grant are not claimed. Refunds and deposits, packages and coupons, tax and invoices, and reconciliation remain PAY-003, PAY-004, PAY-005 and PAY-006.

Evidence: `tests/payments-wire.test.mjs`, `tests/booking-wire/payment-journey.mjs`, `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs`, migration `0068_stripe_payments.sql`, screenshots and `stripe-test-mode.json` in `evidence/contract/2026-10-21/PAY-001/`.

AVAIL-012 is done on the native single-host surface: locked location time and the invitee browser observation stay distinct from request submission through booking, confirmation, manage, export and restore. Operator-created bookings without an invitee observation explicitly say “Zone not provided by invitee.” Existing SMS and workflow-variable obligations remain inherited by FLOW-002 and FLOW-001.

Evidence: `tests/booking-wire/booking-policy-journey.mjs`, `tests/export-base-adoption.test.mjs`, `tests/archive-prior-release.test.mjs`, migration `0067_request_browser_timezone.sql` and screenshots in `evidence/contract/2026-10-21/AVAIL-012/`.

REPORT-001 is partial on the single-host surface: Bookings has a scheduling report for a chosen period and event type with bookings created, rescheduled and canceled (with reasons), attendance and no-show rates, lead time, popular days and hours in the host's zone, bookings by UTM and source and by duration, and revenue. Every figure states its definition and opens the bookings list on exactly the bookings it counts. Host and team choice, routing-form conversion, round-robin distribution and a member seeing only their own figures wait for the clauses that build teams, routing forms and roles; refunds and fees wait for PAY-003.

Evidence: `tests/scheduling-report.test.mjs` with its seeded month in `tests/review/report-month.ts`, and the 1440 and 390 screen proof `scripts/verify-report-screens.mjs`.
