# Dial acceptance

Start with the jobs a new host and invitee need to complete:

- The host signs in, saves a profile and timezone, creates an event with a private location, sets weekly availability, publishes it and copies its booking link.
- An invitee opens that link without an account, picks a time in their own zone, confirms with a name and email, and gets the location, an add-to-calendar file and a private manage link.
- The invitee reschedules or cancels through that private link, and the host sees the change in Bookings, with list times in the host's named zone.
- An invitee requests another time; the host reads the windows in both zones and creates the agreed booking from the request.
- In local development, `rer dev` prints the named owner and the `RER_OPEN` URL for automatic Base development sign-in; a fresh owner opens `/setup` and a completed owner opens `/app`, without sign-in mail or a captured-mail inbox. Local outgoing mail is validated and suppressed; it proves no real delivery.
- On a hosted install, deploy claims the owner and the owner signs in by email link. Working mail and a readable owner mailbox are prerequisites. The owner can export their data and remove the deployment using the documented commands.

The checks and provider limits below support those jobs.

The target contract widens those jobs to the whole scheduling category; a build round works towards these through the ledger below:

- A team admin invites hosts, connects each host's Google, Microsoft 365 or iCloud calendars and Zoom or Teams, publishes round-robin, collective and group events from a managed template, and sees fair distribution.
- A website visitor fills a routing form, is routed by their answers or their CRM owner to the right host, pays a deposit through the host's Stripe or PayPal, and gets email and consented SMS reminders.
- After the meeting, attendance is established, a no-show is recovered under the host's policy, and the agreed follow-up is drafted for the host to approve; HubSpot, Salesforce or Orbit and Slack see the outcome.
- A 15-host Calendly team switches: each rep's mail covers only their own bookings, calendar invitations and emails read as the event's templates say, invitees choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes, a booking lands on the team's own thank-you page, SMS reminders work on day one through RER Managed SMS, reps insert times from Outlook or Gmail, and every Calendly setting the API cannot carry is a checklist step.
- The ops admin invites 14 reps and two viewers: reps act only on their own and delegated hosts' bookings and requests, and a rep or viewer who tries to open the owner editor, remove a colleague, erase an invitee, block a domain, define a custom field or start a move-in is refused with nothing changed and the refusal audited.
- An owner moves in from Calendly, Acuity Scheduling or Microsoft Bookings, including teams, routing, workflows and future bookings, without notifying anyone again, and cuts over every old link.


The release candidate must preserve every requirement in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) and [BASELINE.md](BASELINE.md), including deliberate supersessions described in [DIVERGENCE.md](DIVERGENCE.md). The manifest's maturity, capacity estimates and accessibility target remain explicit limits on claims.
